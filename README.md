# The-Strait-
You are a lone ship traveling through a strait somewhere in the world after your carrier strike group was decimated. You still have to complete the mission. Dodge shaheb drones, don't get hit by PT boats and make it to the end of the Strait. 

#### Controls are as follows
A and D to move port and starboard, space to fire the CIWS, and E to fire harpoon missiles. Remember, only a missile can take down a boat, and only a CIWS can take down an attack drone.
